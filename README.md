This project was created for the course [Game Engine Fundamentals](https://digitalartsandentertainment.be/page/45/Game+Engine+Fundamentals) in the bachelor major [Game Development](https://digitalartsandentertainment.be/page/31/Game+Development) at [Digital Arts & Entertainment](https://digitalartsandentertainment.be/).

As the name of the course implies, this was an introduction to the Unity Engine, I personally had no prior experience with the engine. After getting a follow along introduction to the engine of roughly 5 hours & 20 minutes we were told to make a prototype and explore the engine further on our own, this is my take on the assignment.

This isn't intended as a portfolio piece but rather as a personal archive, I like to come back to my previous work and check how I solved a problem in the past before spending precious time researching a new solution.  
Hence why there is no elaborate showcase of what this repository is all about.
